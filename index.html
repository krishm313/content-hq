Content HQ

A personal publishing tool for my own YouTube channels.

Content HQ is software that runs only on my own computer. I use it to plan, review and upload the short videos I make to the YouTube channels I own. It is not offered to anyone else.

When I connect a channel, Content HQ uses the YouTube API Services to upload my finished videos (with their title, description, tags and publish time) and to read back their upload status.

Content HQ asks Google for two permissions only:

  • Upload videos: to send finished videos to my channel.
  • View your YouTube account: to show the channel's name and check an upload's status.

It cannot edit or delete existing videos, and it does not read comments, analytics or anyone else's data.
